🚆 BUYING YOUR MEITETSU TICKET
Find the stationCentrair Station is directly below the terminal — go down to B1 and follow signs for "Meitetsu Line / Trains to Nagoya." You can't miss it; it's a short walk from arrivals.
Buy the ticketAt the ticket machines, switch to English, then select "Nagoya" as your destination. You'll be offered two fare types: ¥980 Limited Express, non-reserved/regular car (一般席) — no seat assigned, sit anywhere in a regular car — or a pricier first-class μticket (regular fare + ~¥450 reserved-seat supplement). Your recollection is correct: pick the ¥980 non-reserved option, buy 2 tickets for both of you.
Find your trainAny Meitetsu Limited Express or Rapid Limited Express bound for Nagoya works with this ticket (μSKY trains are reserved-only, so skip those unless you bought a μticket). Check the platform departure board for the next one — they run frequently.
Board (non-reserved)On the platform, look for car markings — first-class/reserved cars are labelled 一般車 vs the reserved car (often the front car, marked with a different colour/symbol on the platform door signage). Board any car NOT marked as the reserved/first-class car. Ride to the last stop, Meitetsu Nagoya Station (~28–37 min).
To the hotelAt Meitetsu Nagoya Station, follow underground signs toward Nagoya Station's Sakura-dori/Meieki side, then take Miyako Underground Mall Exit 4 up to street level. ibis Styles Nagoya is about a 2-minute walk from that exit (~8 min total walk from the station).

Matcha Run + Nintendo

FRI 30 OCT 2026 · Kyoto - Uji
Don't forget! Stock up on matcha & special snacks from Uji/Kyoto today — this is THE matcha day.

Route order — one straight line down the JR Nara Line, no backtracking ① Kanbayashi Shunsho Honten (Kyoto Isetan B1F) → ② Tamakitei (Ōbaku) → ③–⑥ Uji Bridge / Byōdō-in cluster: Nakamura Tokichi (Byōdō-in branch), Torikiku, Ocha no Kanbayashi (Ujibashidori), Horii Shichimeien → ⑦ Marukyu Koyamaen Main Plant (Ogura) → ⑧ Nintendo Museum → ⑨ Enen Yakiniku (dinner, back in Kyoto).

Getting to Tamakitei Ride the local JR Nara Line straight from Kyoto Station to Ōbaku Station (~28 min, no transfer). The faster "Miyakoji Rapid" trains skip Ōbaku entirely, so local is the only direct option — it costs the same fare either way. Tamakitei is a ~5–8 min walk from the station.

Suggested timing 10:00 Kanbayashi Shunsho → 10:20 depart Kyoto (local train) → 10:48 Ōbaku, walk to Tamakitei → ~11:05–11:20 Tamakitei → taxi ~2–3km to Uji Bridge/Byōdō-in (~10–15 min, faster than backtracking by train) → 11:20–12:15 tea-shop cluster → walk to Uji Sta. → JR Nara Line 1 stop to Ogura (~2–4 min) → 12:30–1:00 Marukyu Koyamaen → 1:00–1:10 walk to Nintendo Museum. That puts you at the Museum by ~1:10 PM, well ahead of the 2:45 PM checkpoint for your 3:00 PM entry — a comfortable buffer for queues or a sit-down lunch.
🛂 IMMIGRATION CHECK
No immigration today — Kyoto/Uji is a domestic day trip within Japan. Just bring your passport along; some matcha shops may ask to see it for tax-free purchases.

🚨 BOSS WARNING — TAX RULE CHANGE!

Japan's tax-free system changes on 1 Nov 2026. Shopping on 29–30 Oct uses the current system (instant exemption at checkout, item sealed in a bag — don't open until you're out of Japan, carry as hand luggage for the customs check before security). But today (3 Nov) is after the changeover — at AEON Mall and other stores you'll likely pay full price (incl. tax) and then need to claim your refund at a counter at Centrair Airport before departure tomorrow. Keep ALL receipts from today's shopping!
